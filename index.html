A Book of Mormon word study

Coming Closer to Christ

An exploration of Christ-centered words and themes across the books of the Book of Mormon.

About this project

The coming closer to Christ project is designed to help students develop faith in Christ, deepen their understanding of the gospel message, and build something that is meaningful to them personally. My goal for this project was to be able to combine my faith with my academic interests. I wanted to create something that helped me develop my skills while also learning about Christ and His teachings.

My project explores how Christ-centered themes appear across the books of the Book of Mormon. I used AntConc to search for selected words, organized and compared the results in Excel, and used Tableau Public to create interactive visualizations. Rates per 10,000 words help compare books of different lengths; higher values show that a searched word or theme appears more often relative to a book’s length. These patterns show word frequency, while the meaning of each term still depends on its context.

Different themes are reported as a combination of different tenses and forms of a central word. For example, the results for "joy" include words such as joy, joyous, rejoice, and rejoicing. The theme Christ's identity and titles contains words such as savior, redeemer, messiah, and lord. This is one reason that it appears as the most prominent theme in the data. The other reason is that the Book of Mormon is centered on Christ. It is appropriate that His names are the most prominent words as He is the most important figure in the book.

As I worked on this project, I came to see how Christ truly is the central theme of the Book of Mormon. I appreciated being able to see the recurring themes of this powerful book. I especially appreciated how present Christ is in each section.

The visualizations compare search-term frequencies. Rates are normalized per 10,000 words so books of different lengths can be compared.
