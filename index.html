A Book of Mormon word study

Coming Closer to Christ

An exploration of Christ-centered words and themes across the books of the Book of Mormon.

About this project

This project explores how Christ-centered themes appear across the books of the Book of Mormon. I used AntConc to search for selected words, organized and compared the results in Excel, and used Tableau Public to create interactive visualizations. Rates per 10,000 words help compare books of different lengths; higher values show that a searched word or theme appears more often relative to a book’s length. These patterns show word frequency, while the meaning of each term still depends on its context.

As I worked on this project, I came to see how Christ truly is the central theme of the Book of Mormon. I appreciated being able to see the recurring themes of this powerful book. I especially appreciated how present Christ is in each section.

The visualizations compare search-term frequencies. Rates are normalized per 1,000 words so books of different lengths can be compared.
